## LCI-0001 | Rajkot hospital surveillance case

**Recording context:** institutional healthcare surveillance. **Primary category:** LC-01. **Acquisition:** A1 (claimed unauthorised infrastructure access), `official_allegation_reported`. **Circulation:** commercial promotion/distribution attributed to investigators. **Response:** reported FIR, arrests and chargesheets.

1. **Acquisition, date unestablished (EV-001).** Newspaper investigation summarises chargesheet and police attribution to unauthorised external CCTV access. The original charging document and any dispositive judgment were not inspected in this pilot.
2. **Distribution, date unestablished (EV-002).** Police allegations reported in journalism described promotional video-hosting use and distribution through messaging services. Neither total profits nor the completeness of this operation is independently established.
3. **17 February 2025 (EV-003).** Reporting says an FIR was registered.
4. **18 February 2025 (EV-004).** Investigation reportedly identified public video-hosting uploads.
5. **19 February 2025 (EV-005).** Initial arrests announced.
6. **17 May 2025 (EV-006).** Reporting of initial chargesheet.
7. **12 September 2025 (EV-007).** Reporting of supplementary chargesheet.

**What the evidence supports:** A documented investigation into institutional recording exposure and alleged commercial distribution. **What it does not:** a verified court determination of a specific defendant's guilt, a technical exploit independently reproduced, or a nationwide estimate of similar operations.

**Sources:** SB-001, SB-002.

## LCI-0002 | Thiruvananthapuram state cinema CCTV episode

**Recording context:** surveillance footage associated with three state cinemas. **Primary category:** LC-01. **Acquisition:** A0 (unknown/unestablished). **Circulation:** originally documented by investigative journalism. **Response:** safeguards reported, FIR registered, investigation still under oversight in October 2026.

1. **Recording source, date unestablished (EV-008).** TNM traced specific footage to cinema CCTV environments.
2. **Circulation, date unestablished (EV-009).** TNM documented promotional and commercial circulation. The precise initial leak time was not established.
3. **1 December 2025 (EV-010).** Original investigation published; this is a publication date, not an acquisition date.
4. **3 December 2025 (EV-011).** Theatre management described changes to connectivity, access and passwords. The date is the *report* date; precise implementation dates may differ.
5. **5 December 2025 (EV-012).** Later reporting says police registered an FIR following a complaint.
6. **23 October 2026 (EV-013).** Commission requested supervision of the continuing investigation; reporting the next day said the precise access mechanism and responsible persons had not been established.

**What the evidence supports:** circulating cinema CCTV footage, an institutional response and continued investigation. **What it does not:** a confirmed hacked cloud account, insider theft, identity of offender, or successful elimination of commercial circulation.

**Sources:** SB-003 to SB-007.

## Comparison

Both are documented surveillance footage exposure episodes with reported commercial dissemination and official response. Only Rajkot has a detailed public report attributing unauthorised system access to particular suspects via police/chargesheet allegations. Kerala has publicly documented uncertainty over access mechanism extending to October 2026. This supports a **methodological finding**: consistent coding is possible despite different evidence completeness. It does not demonstrate which pathway is more common in India.

## Minimum changes before future publication

- Independently retrieve judicial documents where available and audit each event's source locator.
- Add source-level extraction notes for finer time precision and contradictory accounts.
- Have an independent reader recode the two pilots to assess category clarity.
- Expand across LC-02 to LC-04 only if corresponding case evidence is located and screened.
