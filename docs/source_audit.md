# Source Bank v0.1 | Audit and source limitations

**As of 26 October 2026:** seven incident-evidence records and two policy/platform contextual records have been reviewed from stable public-facing publications. IDs are the keys in `data/sources.csv`.

| Source ID | Episode | Role | Key use and limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| SB-001 | Rajkot | Detailed original investigation | Reports police interview/chargesheet-derived allegations of unauthorised CCTV access, distribution, arrests and chargesheet filings. Chargesheet itself was not separately obtained. |
| SB-002 | Rajkot | Earlier reporting | Confirms what police said about initial FIR, seventh arrest and alleged circulation. Not independent evidence of guilt. |
| SB-003 | Kerala theatres | Original investigative reporting | Reporter directly traced footage from three cinemas and described offers of commercial circulation. Does not identify acquisition mechanism. |
| SB-004 | Kerala theatres | Institutional response | Quotes theatre management describing mitigation. Effectiveness not established by independent audit. |
| SB-005 | Kerala theatres | FIR reporting | States 5 December 2025 complaint/FIR and its allegations. Does not settle how footage was acquired. |
| SB-006 | Kerala theatres | Competing hypotheses | Reports both potential intrusion and insider/system leakage possibilities. These are hypotheses, not findings. |
| SB-007 | Kerala theatres | Oversight update | PTI reporting on 2026 Human Rights Commission direction. Explicitly states origin/person remained unresolved. |
| SB-008 | Context only | Government policy | MeitY NCII procedure. No individual incident claim supported. |
| SB-009 | Context only | Platform statement | Meta's statements on advertising enforcement. Self-reported; not an independent platform audit. |

**Dependence cautions:** SB-001 and SB-002 largely trace the same police investigation. SB-003, SB-004 and SB-005 are follow-ups by the same publication but on distinct later actions. SB-007 is a PTI account of a Commission statement, not the original signed order.

**Research gaps:** Obtain original judicial records/charge filings if lawfully accessible, confirm status changes after October 2026, and locate independently documented incidents outside surveillance premises before broadening the schema. Avoid inferring all distribution events belong to one national criminal organisation.
