## Remaining research limitations

- Not a prevalence sample of India.
- Cases are primarily based on published police descriptions rather than underlying FIRs or trial judgments.
- Source access and credibility checks are documentary checks, not independent verification of police allegations.
- Technical platform intrusion is not established for any new case by the material used.
- Secondary source lineages must not be counted as independent corroboration.
- Pilot reconstructed events are ordered analytically when original dates are unavailable; graph arrows do not establish causation.
- Independent second-reviewer coding remains pending.
