# LeakChain India | Research protocol v0.1

**Study design:** purposive, document-based comparative event reconstruction using publicly available safe published sources and source-attributed claims.

**Primary objective:** evaluate feasibility of a reproducible data model that records origin context, documented/claimed acquisition, circulation stages, institutional response and remaining unknowns in Indian intimate-imagery exposure incidents.

**Pilot cases:** one hospital CCTV investigation in Rajkot, Gujarat, and one cinema CCTV episode linked to three theatres in Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala. Deliberately contrasting available evidence levels. No prevalence estimation.

**Unit:** one underlying episode of exposure/distribution; nested events and source-specific claims. The unit is not one victim, one account, one uploaded video, or one news article.

**Source method:** search public official documents and original journalism, register published URL, publisher, date, event relevance, dependencies and limitations. Record only safe article URLs, not exploited content locations. Check primary source where feasible; if access is unavailable, explicitly note reporting of its content.

**Analysis:** apply v0.1 incident/acquisition categories, reconstruct events without inventing missing dates, compare observed stages and gaps. Model links are display-only, not proven transmission or causal edges. Findings are exploratory and conditional on documented sources.

**Quality assurance:** codebook-defined enums, foreign-key integrity validation, repeat incident deduplication, source-to-claim traceability, potential second-reader recoding, versioned amendments.

**Research limitations:** media-report selection, inconsistent disclosure, no original digital exhibits, unresolved technical cause in Kerala, and uncertain outcomes in Rajkot. No conclusions about national rates, efficacy of moderation, or persistent trans-platform organisations from these two cases.

**Ethical/public release boundary:** never collect or publish intimate recordings, victim identities, imagery previews, seller handles, active links, or intrusion details. Public records are not blanket permission to re-identify affected people.

**Planned next milestone:** 10-12 screened episodes spanning LC-01 through LC-04; separate candidate/exclusion log; further codebook calibration and independent review before public data expansion.
